# Insights Summary (Jan 2023 – Dec 2024)

**Headline:** ₹24.11 Cr sales, ₹2.88 Cr profit (11.9 % margin), 8,947 orders, 1,595 customers, average order ₹26,942.

1. **Growth:** sales rose from ₹11.39 Cr (2023) to ₹12.71 Cr (2024), **+11.6 % YoY**.
2. **Festive peak:** October + November alone deliver **22.3 %** of annual sales (≈11 % each vs ≈7.5 % in a normal month). Stock, staffing and marketing should be planned around these months.
3. **Technology dominates revenue but not margin:** 71.8 % of sales at only 10.3 % margin; Fashion is 2.5 % of sales at 30.5 % margin and Office Supplies 26.1 %. Growing high-margin categories would lift overall profit more than pushing laptops.
4. **Top products:** Laptop Pro 16 (₹7.8 Cr) and Laptop Air 14 (₹5.0 Cr) are the top two; the top 10 products deliver ~84 % of sales – high concentration risk.
5. **Discounts hurt profit:** margin is 14.6 % at 0 % discount, 10.6 % at 1–10 %, 5.1 % at 11–20 %, and **−1.7 % above 20 %**. Discounts over 20 % lose money.
6. **Regions:** North (26.1 %), South (25.7 %) and West (25.3 %) are near equal; East 15.2 %; Central 7.7 % – the clearest expansion gap.
7. **Customers:** the top 20 % of customers generate ~51 % of sales; Consumer segment = 58 %, Corporate 28 %, Home Office 14 %.
8. **Payments:** UPI is the leading mode (35.9 %), COD still 15.8 % – a lever for reducing COD-related returns/cost.

*Note: figures come from the synthetic dataset; the method, not the absolute numbers, is what carries over to real data.*
