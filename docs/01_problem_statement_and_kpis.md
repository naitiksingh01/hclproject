## 1. Problem statement
A multi-category online/offline retailer sells across 18 Indian states (5 regions). Raw order-line exports arrive with duplicates, missing values, refund entries, wrong totals and mixed date formats, so management cannot trust the monthly sales number or see which products, regions and categories drive revenue and profit.

**Goal:** build an end-to-end pipeline – *Python cleaning → Oracle star-schema → Power BI dashboard* – that produces a trusted, reconciled sales view with KPIs, slicers and drill-down, and documents every data-quality decision.

**Business questions answered**
1. How much did we sell and earn, and how is it trending month by month / year over year?
2. Which products, categories and regions contribute most – and which have weak margin?
3. Does discounting hurt profitability?
4. How concentrated is revenue among customers, payment modes and segments?

## 2. KPI definitions (8 KPIs; acceptance needs ≥ 5)
| # | KPI | Definition | Power BI measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Total Sales | Σ `sales` where sales = qty × unit_price × (1 − discount) | `Total Sales` |
| 2 | Total Profit | Σ `profit` | `Total Profit` |
| 3 | Profit Margin % | Total Profit ÷ Total Sales | `Profit Margin %` |
| 4 | Orders | Distinct `order_id` | `Total Orders` |
| 5 | Average Order Value | Total Sales ÷ Orders | `Avg Order Value` |
| 6 | Unique Customers | Distinct `customer_id` excluding `UNKNOWN` | `Unique Customers` |
| 7 | Units Sold | Σ `quantity` | `Units Sold` |
| 8 | Sales MoM / YoY Growth % | (current − previous period) ÷ previous period | `Sales MoM %`, `Sales YoY %` |

## 3. Scope & assumptions
* Period Jan-2023 → Dec-2024, currency ₹.
* Refund/negative rows are **excluded** from Sales (they are quarantined in `reports/rejected_rows.csv`, not silently deleted).
* Rows with an impossible order date are quarantined (cannot be placed on a timeline).
* Bulk orders (qty ≥ 40) are real orders: **kept and flagged** (`is_bulk_order`).
* Dataset is **synthetic** (generated by `python/01_generate_dataset.py`, seed 42) with realistic dirty patterns, because the project was built without internet access to Kaggle. The pipeline works unchanged on a real Kaggle retail CSV with the same column names.
